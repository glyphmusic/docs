# First steps

On a fresh install, Glyph opens with a short **welcome tour** that introduces
search, your library, sound, lyrics, sync, performance settings (picking full
effects or performance mode based on your device) and colour themes. You can
reopen it any time under **Settings › About › Introduction**.

1. **Search.** Press `Ctrl+K` (or click Search) and type a song, an artist or
   even a line of the lyrics. Pasting a playlist or song link adds or imports it.
   Coming from another service? [Transfer your library](../using-glyph/transfer-music.md).
2. **Play.** Click a song. Right-click it for more: play next, add to the
   queue, add to a playlist, download, go to the artist.
3. **Like and collect.** The heart saves a song to *Liked songs*; right-click a
   heart (on a phone: press and hold) to tick *Liked songs* and any playlist at
   once. Playlists, followed artists and saved albums live in the *Library* and
   in the sidebar. Your own music files are under **Library › Local files**.
4. **Now playing.** Open the full-screen view for the cover, synced lyrics and the
   queue. Press `Z` for zen mode: only the cover and the player bar. See
   [Now Playing](../using-glyph/now-playing.md).
5. **Make it yours.** Change any [keyboard shortcut](keyboard-shortcuts.md),
   the [text size and background](../using-glyph/appearance.md), or scroll
   over the seek and volume bars with the mouse wheel.

## Handy controls

Most buttons in the player bar have a right-click menu (on a phone: press and
hold):

| Button | Right-click (or press and hold) |
| --- | --- |
| Heart | save the song to *Liked songs*, one or more playlists, or a new playlist |
| Previous / next | jump 10 or 30 seconds, restart the song |
| Shuffle | in order, smart shuffle or fully random |
| Repeat | off, repeat all, repeat one |
| Volume | choose the audio output, mono / stereo / spatial, MPD |
| Mini player | open it as a card or as a bar |

Right-clicking an entry in the sidebar lets you pin it or hide it.

## Languages

Glyph speaks English, Afrikaans, Deutsch, Español, Français, Italiano, Polski,
Português, Русский, Türkçe and Українська. It follows your system language; change it under
**Settings › Appearance**. Want to add one? See [Translations](../contributing/translations.md).
