# Lyrics

Synced lyrics come from LRCLIB. The current line stays in the middle of the view,
moves smoothly and lights up as it is sung.

## Wrong lyrics?

Open the lyrics options (the source button above the lyrics) and pick another **version**. Glyph
lists every candidate it finds, synced and plain, and remembers your choice for
that song.

## Timing

If the lyrics run early or late, shift them in steps under **Timing** in the same
panel. *Reset* returns to the original timing.

## Views

*Song + lyrics* shows the cover next to the lyrics; *Lyrics* fills the window.
