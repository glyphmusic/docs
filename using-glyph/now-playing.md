# Now Playing and the player bar

Click the cover in the player bar, or press `Ctrl+L`, to open **Now Playing**.

## Views

The switch above the lyrics picks the view:

| View | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| Song + lyrics | the cover with its own controls, the lyrics next to it |
| Lyrics | only the lyrics, centred; the player bar stays at the bottom |
| Zen | only the big cover and the player bar: no sidebar, no search bar, no lyrics |

**Zen mode** also starts with `Z` or the expand button (two arrows) under the
cover.
`Esc`, `Z` or the button in the top right corner end it. Leaving Now Playing
ends it too.

**Hide the lyrics** with `Ctrl+L` while Now Playing is open. Glyph keeps that
choice after a restart; **Settings › Playback › Player › Lyrics in Now
Playing** switches it as well.

## Buttons under the cover

Shuffle, previous, play, next, repeat, the volume, like and the sleep timer,
and two more:

* **Download** saves the playing song for offline use. While it downloads the
  button shows a ring, then a pink check. `Ctrl+D` does the same while no
  text field is focused. Hide the button under **Settings › Playback › Player › Download
  button in Now Playing**.
* **Zen mode** (two arrows).

## Mouse wheel on the player bar

Scroll over the **seek bar** to jump forward (up) or back (down), and over the
**volume** to make it louder or quieter. This works in the player bar and in
Now Playing.

| Setting (**Settings › Playback › Player**) | Choices | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mouse wheel on the seek bar | off, 1, 5, 10, 15 or 30 seconds per notch | 5 s |
| Mouse wheel on the volume | off, 1, 2, 5 or 10 % per notch | 5 % |

On a touchpad, every short swipe is one step.
