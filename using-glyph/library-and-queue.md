# Library, playlists and queue

## Library

*Liked songs*, *Recently played*, your playlists, followed artists, saved albums
and downloads. Everything can be pinned to the sidebar; right-click a sidebar
entry to hide it again.

## Playlists

* Create one with **New playlist**: type a name, or paste a link or open a
  playlist file to [import](transfer-music.md) it. Add songs from any
  right-click menu, or drag music files from your computer into a playlist.
* Names can be up to 100 characters. A cover image is optional.
* **Share**: copy a short `glyph.my` link (signed in) or a `glyph:playlist:` code.
* **Download all** saves the playlist for offline use where the source allows it;
  *Automatically download new songs* keeps it complete.
* **Sort** (the arrows on a list page): title, artist, album, length or the
  list's own order. Glyph remembers it per list, and *Play* follows the shown
  order. Songs can only be dragged into a new order while a playlist shows its
  own order.
* **Where it is stored** (signed in; right-click a playlist): *Local playlist*
  stays on this computer only, *Account playlist* syncs to all your devices,
  *Account + offline* also downloads it. The playlist page shows which one it
  is. Without an account every playlist is local.

## Queue

The queue has three parts:

| Section | Contains |
| --- | --- |
| Now playing | the current song |
| Up next | songs you added with *Play next* or *Add to queue*; they always come first |
| Next from: *playlist* | the rest of the list you started |

Click any song in the queue to continue from there. When the queue ends, radio
can continue with similar songs (needs a Last.fm key on the server or in your
settings). They are based on the last few songs, join the end of the queue (so
*Previous* still goes back into the list) and leave out what just played.

A song that cannot play is skipped with a short note; after five in a row Glyph
stops and says so.

Removing a song from a playlist, *Liked songs*, *Recently played* or
*Downloads*, or deleting a playlist, shows a toast with **Undo** for a few
seconds.

## Radio

**Start radio** in the right-click menu of a song, playlist, album or artist
(or the radio button on list and artist pages) plays songs similar to it. For a
list Glyph takes up to five songs spread over it, so the radio fits the whole
list; songs already in it are left out and artists are spread out.

## Sleep timer

The timer button (player bar and Now Playing) pauses playback after 15, 30, 45,
60 or 90 minutes, or at the end of the current song. Over the last 30 seconds
the music fades out; the volume is back when you play again. Click the button
again to see the time left or turn it off. The timer is not kept across restarts.

## Shuffle

Two shuffle styles (**Settings › Playback** or right-click the shuffle button):

* **Smart** feels random: songs by the same artist are spread out and songs you
  just heard move to the end.
* **Fully random** gives every order the same chance, repeats included.

With *Repeat all*, every round is shuffled anew and never starts with the song
that just ended.
