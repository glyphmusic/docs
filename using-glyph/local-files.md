# Local files

Glyph plays music files from folders on your computer: MP3, FLAC, OGG, M4A,
AAC, WAV and MKA. Open **Library › Local files** (desktop).

{% hint style="info" %}
Files play from where they are. Glyph does not copy them, move them or upload
them anywhere, and the list of folders stays on this computer: it is neither
synced nor part of shared settings.
{% endhint %}

## Add a folder

1. Open **Library › Local files** and click **Add folder** (or **Settings ›
   Cache & Storage › Local music folders › Open local files**).
2. Pick one or more folders. Subfolders are included, up to 12 levels deep;
   hidden folders (names starting with `.`) are skipped.
3. Glyph reads the tags (title, artist, album) and the embedded cover of each
   file. The page shows how far it is (*Reading files 120 / 800*).

Files without tags get their title and artist from the file name
(`Artist - Title.mp3`, `03 Title.flac`).

**Remove** a folder with the **×** next to it. Its files stay on your disk;
Glyph only forgets them.

## New and changed files

Glyph reads the folders again the first time you open the page after starting
Glyph, and when you click **Rescan**. Only new or changed files are read again, so a
rescan of a large library is quick. Files you deleted disappear from the list.

## Browse

| Tab | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| Folders | your music folders; click one to open it, the path above the list goes back up |
| Artists | every artist with the number of songs |
| Albums | albums from the album tags, with their cover |
| Songs | every song, sorted by artist and album |

The search field filters the open tab by title, artist and album. In an open
folder it searches that folder and every folder below it.

**Play** and **Shuffle** play what the page lists; while it lists folders,
artists or albums they play all your local songs.

## Open with Glyph

Choose **Open with › Glyph** on a music file (MP3, FLAC, OGG, M4A, AAC, WAV or
MKA) on Windows, macOS, Linux or Android. On the computer it plays at once;
several selected files play as one list. **Send to › Glyph** works too, also for
a link dragged out of the browser. On the phone a small window asks whether the
song goes to Liked Songs or a playlist. A song added this way keeps playing from
the file.

## Playlists, likes and other devices

Local songs work like any other song: like them, add them to playlists or the
queue. On another device, where the file does not exist, Glyph looks the song
up by title and artist in your sources instead.

Dragging music files onto a playlist is different: it **copies** them into
Glyph's own folder, so they stay playable even if you delete the originals.

## Not finding your files?

* The format must be one of the formats above.
* Click **Rescan** after copying new files.
* Files that cannot be read are counted in a short message after the scan.
