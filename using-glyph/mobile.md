# Android app

Glyph runs on Android 8 or newer with a layout made for phones and native
background playback. See [Install](../getting-started/install.md#android-preview)
to download the APK and keep it updated.

## Touch gestures and shortcuts

* **Save to a playlist from the heart:** press and hold any heart icon (in
  lists, the mini player bar or the full-screen player) to open the **Save to**
  menu. Tick Liked Songs or one or more playlists, or tap **New playlist**.
* **Right-click menus on phones:** pressing and holding other player buttons
  (such as previous, next, shuffle, repeat or volume) opens the same extra
  menus desktop users get with a right-click.
* **Queue swipes:** in the queue, swipe a song to the **right** to add it to
  the queue again, or to the **left** to remove it. Songs you added yourself
  show a small queue mark in the accent colour before the artist name.
* **Jump to the current list:** in the full-screen player, tap **Playing from …**
  at the top to open that playlist, album, Liked Songs or artist.
* **Bulk remove:** in Liked Songs or your own playlists, tap the select button
  (circle with a check) to pick several songs and remove them in one step.

## Share menu and opening files

* **Share from Glyph:** tapping **Share** on a song, playlist, album, artist,
  Jam invite or yearly recap opens a bottom sheet with the cover, link and
  buttons for **Copy link**, **WhatsApp**, **Telegram**, **SMS**, **E-mail**
  and **More** (Android's own share menu).
* **Share to Glyph without leaving the other app:** share a Spotify, YouTube,
  Deezer, SoundCloud or Glyph link (or a music file) to Glyph from another app.
  The **Add to Glyph** sheet opens right over the app you are in, so you can
  pick Liked Songs, one or more playlists, **Save as playlist** or **Play**,
  then go straight back where you were. Nothing is added without asking.
* **Open with Glyph:** opening an MP3, FLAC, OGG, M4A, AAC, WAV or MKA file
  with Glyph opens the same sheet. Songs added this way keep playing from the
  file.

## Notifications

Glyph can notify you on Android when:

* a **new version** of Glyph is ready to install (tap to open the update page),
* an **artist you follow** puts out a new album, EP or single (several releases
  at once are grouped into one notification; tap to open the release).

Glyph checks about twice a day in the background, even while the app is closed.
Switch either kind off under **Settings › Notifications**, or manage the two
channels directly in Android's app notification settings.

## Background playback and offline mode

* Audio plays through a native Android media service, so music keeps playing
  with the screen off and responds to lock-screen, Bluetooth and headphone
  controls. The Glyph note icon appears in the status bar while music plays.
* When you lose your connection, the offline bar links straight to
  **Downloads**. Downloaded songs, local files and cached audio keep playing;
  previously opened artist pages and albums still show their cached copy.

## What stays desktop-only

A few desktop system features do not apply on phones: local music folder
scanning, autostart / system tray, Discord Rich Presence, MPD output and
rebindable desktop keyboard shortcuts. Everything else (including end-to-end
encrypted sync, Connect, Jam, equalizer, crossfade, volume levelling, downloads
and plugins) works on Android too.
